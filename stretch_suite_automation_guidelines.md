A reference document for writing consistent, on-brand email and SMS automations for Stretch Suite.

## Target Audience

- Women and men 50+ from Oswego County
- Also serves athletes, desk workers, manual laborers, and chronic pain sufferers
- Always write with equal respect for all genders — fully gender-neutral language throughout
- The audience values practicality, comfort, and independence — not fitness trends or intensity

## Key Differentiators to Reinforce

- Sessions are fully clothed — clients wear everyday clothes or stretchy workout clothing; no special gear needed; grip socks are recommended but optional
- One-on-one private sessions — not a class, not a group setting
- No experience or flexibility required — truly accessible to everyone
- The experience feels like practical care for the body, not a workout
- First dedicated assisted stretch studio in the Oswego area

---

## Tone of Voice

- **Warm, reassuring, and supportive** — never pushy, urgent, or salesy
- **Practical and grounded** — speak to how people actually feel in their body (stiffness, limited range, discomfort), not abstract fitness goals
- **Encouraging without being motivational-poster-y** — no "crush your goals" energy
- **Conversational and clear** — short sentences, plain language, accessible to all literacy levels
- **Respectful of age and experience** — never condescending, never assumes a level of fitness
- Do not use clinical or overly technical language
- Do not use fitness industry jargon or trend language

---

## Copy Rules (Apply to All Automations)

### Hard Rules — Never Break
- **No em-dashes** anywhere (not even in subject lines or button text)
- **No phone number** in any copy — phone is for inquiries only and should not appear in automations
- **No heart emojis** (no hearts in any form)
- **Emojis in texts:** none — zero emojis in SMS copy
- **Emojis in emails:** use very sparingly; the only approved emoji is 🌿; use at most once per email and only when it genuinely fits
- **Fully gender-neutral language** throughout — no "ladies," "guys," "men," "women," "him," "her," etc.; use "you," "your," "they," "them," "clients," "people," "everyone"
- **All booking must go through stretchsuite.com/booking** — never instruct someone to call or email to book
- No exclamation marks overuse — limit to one per email, zero in texts unless tone truly warrants it

### Style Preferences
- Short subject lines — clear and plain, not clever for the sake of it
- Keep SMS under 160 characters when possible; always under 320
- Email CTAs should be a single, clear action (usually "Book Your Session" or similar)
- Open with something that speaks to how the person feels, not what Stretch Suite does
- Avoid filler phrases: "We wanted to reach out," "Just checking in," "Hope this finds you well"

## Booking and Contact Guidelines

- **Booking:** Always link directly to stretchsuite.com/booking
- **General inquiries:** contact@stretchsuite.com
