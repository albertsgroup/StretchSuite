# Stretch Suite — Automation Writing Guidelines

A reference document for writing consistent, on-brand email and SMS automations for Stretch Suite.

---

## Business Overview

- **Business name:** Stretch Suite
- **Location:** 155 West First Street, Oswego NY 13126
- **Website:** stretchsuite.com
- **Booking:** stretchsuite.com/booking (online only — all sessions must be booked here)
- **Inquiries:** contact@stretchsuite.com (email only — no phone number in any copy)

---

## Brand Voice

- **Tone:** Warm, personable, grounded, and practical
- **Feel:** Reassuring and supportive without being gushing or overly familiar
- **Approach:** Knowledgeable but never clinical — speak like a trusted person in the community, not a salesperson
- **Pacing:** Calm and unhurried — never pushy, never urgent

---

## Audience

- Women and men 50+ from Oswego County
- Also serves athletes, desk workers, manual laborers, and chronic pain sufferers
- Always write with equal respect for all genders — fully gender-neutral language throughout
- The audience values practicality, comfort, and independence — not fitness trends or intensity

---

## Key Differentiators to Reinforce

- Sessions are **fully clothed** — clients wear everyday clothes or stretchy workout clothing; no special gear needed; grip socks are recommended but optional
- **One-on-one private sessions** — not a class, not a group setting
- **No experience or flexibility required** — truly accessible to everyone
- The experience feels like practical care for the body, not a workout
- First dedicated assisted stretch studio in the Oswego area

---

## Copy Rules

### Always
- Use **gender-neutral language** throughout — no he/she assumptions
- Direct all booking to **stretchsuite.com/booking**
- Keep emails warm, concise, and easy to read
- Write SMS messages as short, direct, and conversational
- Sign off emails as **The Stretch Suite Team**
- Include address and email in the footer of the first and last email in any sequence, and any standalone transactional emails

### Never
- Use **em-dashes** — anywhere, ever
- Include the **phone number** (315-905-0155) in any marketing copy — it is for inquiries only and should not appear in automations
- Use **heart emojis** of any kind
- Use **more than one emoji** in any single email
- Use **any emoji in SMS/text messages**
- Use overly familiar or "touchy feely" phrases such as:
  - "We've been thinking about you"
  - "We'd love to see you again"
  - "We miss you"
  - Any language that sounds inappropriately personal for a wellness business
- Present phone or email as a **booking channel** — booking is online only

### Emojis
- **Emails:** Use very sparingly — one per email at most, only when it adds warmth
- **Texts:** No emojis ever
- **Approved emoji:** 🌿 only

---

## Formatting Guidelines

### Emails
- Short paragraphs — two to three sentences max
- Use bullet points for step-by-step instructions or feature lists
- No bold or heavy formatting in the body — keep it clean and readable
- Always include a clear next step (usually the booking link)
- Subject lines: clear and benefit-driven, occasionally a light touch of warmth

### SMS / Texts
- One to two sentences maximum
- Always end with the booking link: stretchsuite.com/booking
- No emojis
- Conversational but concise — not robotic, not overly casual

## Sequence Tone by Stage

| Stage | Tone Guidance |
|---|---|
| New leads (early) | Welcoming, informative, low pressure — introduce the experience |
| New leads (mid) | Helpful and reassuring — address hesitation, answer unspoken questions |
| New leads (late) | Gentle nudge — remind them the door is open, keep it simple |
| Post first session | Encouraging and grounded — acknowledge the milestone, motivate consistency |
| Milestone sessions (10, 25, 50) | Celebratory but not over the top — recognize commitment, point to next goal |
| Lapsed 30 days | Practical check-in — acknowledge the gap without guilt |
| Lapsed 60 days | Reassuring — remind them how easy it is to return |
| Lapsed 120 days | Open door — no pressure, just presence |
| Lapsed 180 days | Final reach out — warm, brief, no guilt, leave the door open |
