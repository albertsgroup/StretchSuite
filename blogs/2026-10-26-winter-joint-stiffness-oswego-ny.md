## Why Do Joints Hurt More in Cold Weather?

Cold slows circulation and thickens the fluid that lubricates your joints, so everything moves with more friction. Muscles also tighten defensively, which pulls on already sensitive joints and shortens your natural range of motion.

This usually shows up first thing in the morning or after sitting still. Gentle, consistent movement keeps that fluid moving and those muscles loose, which is exactly what we focus on in every session. ## Why Is Winter Especially Hard on Mobility in Oswego, NY?

Oswego sits right in the lake-effect snow belt, so cold temperatures and winter inactivity hit at the same time. Less daily movement combined with colder, tighter muscles is a fast track to stiffness for anyone in Central New York.

Snowbound weeks mean fewer walks and more sitting, and that drop in movement is often a bigger factor in winter stiffness than the cold itself. That is why a stretching routine matters more here than in most other parts of the state.

## How Can You Avoid Back Pain From Shoveling Snow?

Warm up your lower back and hamstrings before you pick up a shovel, and take breaks every five to ten minutes to stretch again. Bend at your knees and hips rather than your waist, and push snow instead of lifting heavy scoops.

Cold, tight muscles strain more easily than warm ones, so a few minutes of stretching before you head outside helps protect your back this winter in Oswego, NY.
