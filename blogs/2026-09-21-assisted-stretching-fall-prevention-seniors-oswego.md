## How Does Assisted Stretching Improve Balance?

Assisted stretching improves balance by loosening the muscles around your hips, ankles, and lower back that control stability. A trained practitioner gently guides each stretch further than you could reach alone, safely improving flexibility in the joints your body relies on most to correct itself mid-step.

Research on adults 58 and older found meaningful balance improvements after just 10 weeks of consistent stretching. That is roughly the length of one season, which makes now a great time in Central New York to start before winter sidewalks get slick. Unlike stretching alone at home, assisted sessions let you relax completely while someone else does the guiding, so you reach depths in a stretch your own muscles would normally resist.

## How Often Should You Stretch to Lower Fall Risk?

Most people see the best results stretching two to three times a week, though even once a week with assisted support makes a difference. Consistency matters more than intensity, so a steady rhythm of shorter sessions beats occasional long ones.
