## What is fall prevention stretching and why does it matter after 50?

Fall prevention stretching targets the hips, ankles, and spine, the joints that control balance and step length as we age. Tight muscles in these areas are one of the most common, and most fixable, reasons older adults lose their footing.

Local programs like the Oswego YMCA's "A Matter of Balance" course exist because falls are a real concern for adults in Central New York, especially through icy winters. Flexibility work is a natural complement to those programs, since a joint that cannot move freely cannot react quickly when you stumble.

## How does assisted stretching reduce fall risk?

Assisted stretching improves range of motion in the hips, ankles, and lower back, and it sharpens proprioception, your body's sense of where it is in space. Research on targeted exercise programs shows fall rates can drop by roughly 23 to 25 percent in older adults who stay consistent.

## What stretches help most with balance?

The stretches that help balance the most open up the hips, calves, and ankles, since these joints control step length and stability. Simple moves like hip-flexor releases, calf stretches, and ankle mobility work all support a steadier gait.

You don't need to guess whether you're doing them safely. A guided session takes that worry off your plate, especially if you're managing arthritis or general stiffness.

## How often should seniors stretch for fall prevention?

Most experts recommend stretching two to three times a week at minimum, though daily stretching brings the fastest results. Even 10 to 15 minutes a day can noticeably improve flexibility within a few weeks.

## Is assisted stretching the same as physical therapy for balance?

No, though the two work well together. Physical therapy typically treats a specific injury or diagnosis, while assisted stretching is a proactive, ongoing way to maintain flexibility.

Many Central New York clients use it as the maintenance step after finishing PT, so the gains they built don't slip away.
